Privacy Policy

Groove Metronome (web app and Android app com.groovemetronome.app) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: Groove Metronome does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Microphone

The bass tuner uses the microphone only while you have switched it on. The sound is analysed on your device to find the pitch of your string; it is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can deny the permission and use every other feature.

Your settings

Your settings (tempo, grooves, mix, practice options) are saved only in your device's browser storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files (see GitHub's privacy statement). After the first visit the app works offline.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/0gring/groove-metronome.
