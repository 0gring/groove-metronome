# Samples

All recordings are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain):

- **[Virtuosity Drums](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums)** (Versilian Studios, drummer Austin McMahon):
  `drums/` kick, snare, cross-stick (`rim`), hi-hat, ride, crash, toms. Each hit is a mono blend of the close,
  overhead and room microphones.
- **[Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL)**:

- `drums/`: claps, tambourine, shaker, claves, cowbell, conga
- `ep/`: Yamaha TX81Z "FM Piano" (electric piano), every major third
- `piano/`: Kawai grand piano, every whole tone

Processed for the app: mono, onset-trimmed, shortened with a fade-out, peak-normalized, FLAC.
`manifest.json` lists the files; key samples carry their measured pitch for tuning correction.
